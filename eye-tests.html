The method: the 8-point scan

Simple idea

Beginners look randomly and miss half of the defects. Experts scan the screen area by area (top to bottom, left to right) and, in each area, ask the same 8 questions.

#CheckAsk yourselfTypical defects
1TextIs every word spelled right, in the right language, complete?Typos, mixed languages, cut-off text, placeholder text like "Hello" or "STR_ID_12"
2UnitsAre units consistent and correct?km/h next to MPH, °C next to °F, kPa next to bar, kHz on FM
3Numbers & formatsCan this value exist? Is the format consistent?30 February, 25:10, 00:75, 21:30 AM, comma vs dot, value above max
4ConsistencyIs the same information shown the same way everywhere?Needle 80 but digital 100; bar 35% but text 82%; two different temperatures
5Icons & coloursDoes the icon match its label? Is the colour's meaning right?Fuel icon with "Hello"; warning in green; red value that is normal
6LayoutAnything overlapping, cut off, misaligned, tilted, missing?Needle covers a number; missing "6" on a scale; image tilted; text overflowing
7StateDoes the highlighted/active state match what is happening?Wrong tab highlighted; "Accept" during an active call; Play icon while playing
8Logic & safetyIs this situation possible? Is a warning missing or wrong?Snowflake at 13 °C; charging while driving; ETA in the past; no ice warning at −3 °C

How to say each defect in the interview

Answer pattern: where → what → why → severity

"In the top bar, the date shows 30 February 2021. That date doesn't exist, so the date logic or formatting is wrong. I'd rate it Major because every driver sees it. Before raising it, I'd check the date signal and the requirement for the date format."

Also mention the non-defects

Saying "this looks strange but is actually correct" shows maturity. Example: a song and its album having the same name is not a bug.

50 common HMI defects to look for (your mental checklist)

Text (10)

Typos · mixed languages · missing translation key (TXT_MEDIA_02) · placeholder text · cut-off text without "…" · text overflowing its box · wrong capitalisation · untranslated units · right-to-left not mirrored · wrong font or size

Numbers & units (10)

Impossible date/time · AM/PM with 24 h · seconds/minutes > 59 · value above max / below min · km with mph · °C with °F · kPa with bar · comma vs dot · different precision (21.0 vs 21) · negative range/time

Consistency (8)

Needle vs digital · cluster vs HUD · bar vs percentage · progress vs time · two temperatures · source label vs icon · map arrow vs text vs lane · status bar vs content

Icons & colours (7)

Icon doesn't match label · warning in green · normal value in red · wrong telltale colour · old and new icon styles mixed · missing icon · blurry/pixelated icon

Layout (8)

Overlap · missing scale number · misalignment · tilted/stretched image · element outside the screen · uneven spacing · pop-up covering controls · scroll bar with nothing to scroll

State & logic (7)

Wrong tab highlighted · button for impossible action · play/pause icon wrong · active mode shown as off · conflicting modes both on · warning with no reason · missing warning
